01PROJECT OVERVIEW

THE PROJECT

Creating the
first connection.

This practical project explored how an email marketing journey can begin — with a clear signup experience, an organized subscriber group, and a campaign prepared around a useful resource.

The work was completed as part of the SGB VA Course using a simulated dental clinic scenario. The MailerLite account was set up under the RASAM name, while the signup form and campaign content were created for the clinic scenario.

06CAMPAIGN SCHEDULING

SEND LATER / SCHEDULE SETUP

Timing is part
of the message.

The campaign setup recorded a planned send to all subscribers on a Tuesday morning. The coursework rationale was to reach recipients during a typical weekday morning when they may be more likely to check email and have time to read a short guide.
