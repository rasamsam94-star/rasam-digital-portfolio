EXERCISE 01CALENDAR

A week with
room to focus.

A weekly scheduling scenario for a Singapore-based founder. The task called for time blocks, grouped activities, logical buffer time, recurring events where appropriate, and clear Singapore Time (SGT) labeling.

  • Prioritize and group similar tasks
  • Allow preparation and buffer time
  • Arrange in-person activities geographically
  • Use color-coding and recurring events where useful

04PLAN / PROJECT COORDINATION

OPTIONAL COURSE TASK / PROJECT MANAGEMENT

Turn an idea into
a workable plan.

The project planning exercise developed a six-week plan for an International Women’s Day celebration for a realtor’s VIP clients, with a target guest list of 30.

The charter defines objectives, scope, stakeholders, deliverables, a proposed timeline, estimated budget, risk responses, and success metrics. These are planning targets, not actual event results.

01 / DEFINE

Project charter

Set the purpose, scope, key stakeholders, expected deliverables, and approval stages before the work begins.

02 / SEQUENCE

Timeline & milestones

Break the six-week planning period into concept development, vendor coordination, event preparation, final coordination, and post-event follow-up.

03 / CONTROL

Budget & risk planning

Identify proposed cost categories and risks such as low RSVP rates, vendor delays, venue availability, and budget overruns.
